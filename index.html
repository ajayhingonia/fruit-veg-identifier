    About Smart Produce Vision

    Smart Produce Vision is an AI-based fruit and vegetable identification system powered by TensorFlow.js and a custom Convolutional Neural Network. It delivers a single bilingual identification (English & Hindi) alongside instant nutritional facts and health benefits—processed 100% locally in your browser.

    Created by Team of Class XI Students

    PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya Bakloh • Student Innovation Project under the sub-theme Artificial Intelligence (AI) for Better Life.

    Trained Produce Categories (Tap any item to preview nutrition)

    Single-Decision Bilingual Output

    Filters neural network predictions above a 75% confidence threshold into one clear English & Hindi result with instant nutrition insights.

    100% On-Device Edge AI

    Runs locally inside your phone or computer browser with multi-camera selection—zero images are ever uploaded to external servers.

    Real-World Impact

    Built to assist smart grocery billing, post-harvest produce sorting, visually impaired shoppers, and interactive nutrition education in schools and homes.
