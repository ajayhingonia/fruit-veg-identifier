About ProduceAI

ProduceAI brings instant, private computer vision directly to any web browser. Powered by a custom-trained Convolutional Neural Network and TensorFlow.js, it identifies fresh fruits and vegetables in real time and delivers a single, decisive answer without overwhelming users with complex probability charts.

Single-Decision Output Engine

Filters raw neural network probabilities through a 75% confidence threshold to display one clear, unambiguous name.

100% On-Device Edge AI

All camera frames are processed locally inside your browser—zero images are uploaded to external servers, ensuring speed and privacy.

Multi-Camera & Cross-Platform

Automatically detects all connected webcams, rear lenses, and front cameras with instant one-tap switching.

Real-World Impact

Designed to accelerate smart retail self-checkouts, streamline post-harvest produce sorting, assist visually impaired shoppers, and serve as an interactive nutrition learning tool.

Built with TensorFlow.js & Teachable Machine • On-Device Computer Vision
